The Skills-to-Income Starter Kit

Skills inventory, first-client outreach template, simple pricing guidance, and a checklist for setting up safely — everything you need to go from “I know things” to “I got paid.”

Back to the article

This kit goes with Turn a Lifetime of Skills Into an Online Side Income. Use it to move from “I know things” to “I got paid” — safely and at your own pace.

General educational guidance only. Not tax, legal, or employment advice. Legitimate platforms never require large upfront fees to “unlock earnings.” If something sounds too good to be true, it is.

Part 1 · Skills inventory worksheet

List without judging

Job skills, life skills, hobbies, languages, fixing, teaching, organising, making…

Now circle items where people already ask you for help, or would pay to save time or learn.

Part 2 · Choose your path

  • ☐ Freelancing an existing skill (writing, bookkeeping, admin, consulting…)
  • ☐ Tutoring or coaching on video
  • ☐ Selling handmade / vintage items
  • ☐ A course or guide (see Digital Product Blueprint)
  • ☐ Helpful content over time (blog/video)

I will start with path: _______________ because _______________

Part 3 · Safe setup checklist

  • ☐ Reliable computer and internet
  • ☐ Dedicated email for this work
  • ☐ Payment method I control (PayPal, bank, platform payout) — never send fees to “activate” earnings
  • ☐ Simple profile: who I help, what I do, proof I can do it
  • ☐ Clear boundaries: hours, response time, what I don’t do
  • ☐ Basic record of income for taxes (ask a tax pro what applies to you)
  • ☐ Scam check: no guaranteed riches, no pressure, no large upfront “training” fee required to get paid

Platform notes and buying criteria: Gear & tools guide.

Part 4 · Simple pricing guidance

Starter formula

  • What similar freelancers/tutors charge: $ _______________ to $ _______________
  • My introductory rate (slightly lower to win first reviews): $ _______________
  • My standard rate after 3–5 happy clients: $ _______________
  • How I package it: ☐ hourly ☐ per session ☐ per project ☐ monthly

What result does the client get (in one sentence)?

Price for the result, not for your anxiety. You can raise rates; starting at zero teaches people your time is free.

Part 5 · First-client outreach template

Message you can copy and personalise

Subject: Quick help with [problem] Hi [Name], I’m offering [specific help] for people who need [result]. I’ve spent years [relevant experience], and I’m taking on a few [clients/students] starting this month. If you — or someone you know — needs help with [problem], I’d love to talk for 15 minutes. No pressure. Here’s what a first session looks like: [one sentence]. Introductory rate: [price]. Warmly, [Your name] [Email / phone / profile link]

People I will contact this week (friends, former colleagues, community):

  1. _______________
  2. _______________
  3. _______________
  4. _______________
  5. _______________

Part 6 · First 30 days action plan

  • Week 1: finish inventory + choose path + create profile
  • Week 2: send 5 outreach messages; join 1 relevant community
  • Week 3: complete first paid or practice session; ask for testimonial
  • Week 4: review pricing; book next clients; decide keep / adjust / pause

There is no right way to use this journal. Skip what doesn't fit. Write a sentence or a page. Come back weeks later and add more. The point is not perfection — it is noticing what matters, so your days can start to match it. Pair this with our free Purpose Playbook when you are ready to turn reflection into action.

If writing about the past or future brings up lasting sadness, anxiety, or hopelessness, please talk with someone you trust or your doctor. This journal is a tool for reflection, not a substitute for care.

How to use these pages

  • Print the journal, or write answers in a notebook using these prompts.
  • Try one section a day, or one a week — whatever feels kind.
  • Share a page with family if you wish; keep others private.

Section 1 · Looking back with kindness

A chapter I'm proud of

Describe a season of your life when you felt most useful, alive, or at home in yourself. What made it so?

Hard-won lessons

What did work, family, or life teach you that younger people rarely hear?

People who shaped me

Name three people who changed your path. What would you thank them for today?

Section 2 · Values that still hold

Where my days already match my values

What are you already doing that feels true to those values?

Where the gap is

What feels missing or out of balance right now — without judging yourself for it?

Section 3 · What these years can hold

A good ordinary day

Describe an ideal weekday five years from now — morning to evening. Be specific and ordinary.

Energy and health

How do you want to feel in your body? What small habits would support that?

People and place

Who do you want nearby? What kind of home, neighbourhood, or community feels right?

Contribution and joy

What do you want to give? What do you want purely for pleasure?

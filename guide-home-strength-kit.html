The 60+ Home Strength Starter Kit

A printable 8-week programme with every session laid out, a simple progress log, and joint-friendly modifications for common aches. No gym, no guesswork.

Back to the article

This 8-week programme builds on Simple Strength Training After 60. Two short sessions a week, six moves, chair and wall, optional light weights or bands. No gym. No guesswork.

General information, not medical advice. If you have heart problems, uncontrolled blood pressure, recent surgery, or a chronic condition, talk to your doctor before beginning. Start light. Stop for chest pain, dizziness, or sharp joint pain.

Safety rules (read once, keep forever)

  • Start lighter than you think you need.
  • Two seconds up, two seconds down — no jerking.
  • Breathe out on the effort; never hold your breath.
  • Rest a full day between sessions (e.g. Mon / Thu).
  • Last 2 reps should feel like honest work, not total failure.

The six moves

  1. Sit-to-stands — legs; progress with slower lowers or a weight at the chest.
  2. Wall push-ups — chest, arms, shoulders; feet further back = harder.
  3. Heel raises — calves and ankles.
  4. Standing hip abductions — side leg lifts for hip stability.
  5. Bent-over rows — light weights or bands; flat back, elbows to ribs.
  6. Overhead press — seated or standing; light weights from shoulder to overhead.

Equipment tips: water bottles work at first. For bands and dumbbells, see the gear guide.

Joint-friendly modifications

  • Knees ache: shallower sit-to-stand; raise seat with a firm cushion.
  • Shoulders sensitive: smaller press range; wall push-ups closer to wall.
  • Balance unsure: keep both hands on chair for hip lifts and heel raises.
  • Back sensitive on rows: support one hand on chair, row one arm at a time, hinge less.
  • Low energy: 1 set of each only — still counts.

8-week progression

Weeks 1–2 · Find your baseline

  • 2 sessions / week
  • 1 set × 8–10 reps each move
  • Bodyweight or very light resistance
  • Focus on form and breathing

Weeks 3–4 · Add a little volume

  • 2 sessions / week
  • 2 sets × 8–10 reps
  • Rest 60–90 seconds between sets

Weeks 5–6 · Build capacity

  • 2–3 sessions / week if recovered
  • 2 sets × 10–12 reps
  • Add light weight/band only if 12 reps feel easy

Weeks 7–8 · Consolidate

  • 2–3 sessions / week
  • 2 sets × 10–12 controlled reps
  • Change only one variable at a time (weight OR reps OR tempo)
  • After week 8: repeat the cycle at a slightly higher challenge, or keep this as maintenance
